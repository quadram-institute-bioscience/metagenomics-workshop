De novo assembly: shotgun simulator

Paste or generate a genome, simulate shotgun reads at a chosen coverage, assemble them with a de Bruijn graph in your browser, then print the raw reads for students to cut out and assemble by hand.

1. Genome

A repeat is a segment copied to two places in the genome. When it is longer than the k-mer size, the graph branches and the assembly breaks there.

2. Sequencing and assembly settings

Unticked: every read comes from the forward strand.

Metrics

Read depth along the genome

Dashed line: mean depth. Dips to zero are places where no read starts close enough to bridge the neighbouring reads, so the assembly has to break there.

Contigs on the genome

Each bar is a contig placed on the true genome (known here because we simulated it).
